What is K-Sites?

K-Sites is a comprehensive Suite for Knock-out/down Studies that integrates multiple biological databases and literature mining to help you pick a safe gene to delete.

Multi-Database Evidence QuickGO is always used; you can also select UniProt, NCBI Entrez and MyGene.info. More databases give a more complete pleiotropy score but take longer.
AI-Powered Predictions RAG-based phenotype prediction from scientific literature.
Smart Filtering Pleiotropy scoring to identify specific, safe targets.
Safety Assessment Comprehensive knockout risk evaluation for each gene.
