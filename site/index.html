4 Analysis Parameters
How many genes to show in the report (1-500), safest first. Every gene of the GO term is assessed, so this does not change the run time; the others are listed as "not shown" with their measured knockout result, so nothing is hidden. All genes will be shown (500 is the largest possible report, shown here as the number).
Cutoff on the 0-10 pleiotropy score: genes scoring above it are left out. 0 = only the most specific genes, 10 = keep everything. Learn more
Experimental: only roles proven in lab experiments (strict, but can make poorly studied genes look more specific than they are).
Computational: only predicted roles.
All: every known role (most cautious about pleiotropy).
Decides which measurement the verdict follows: cell-line essentiality (DepMap) or whole-organism knockout survival (MGI, IMPC, SGD, ZFIN, FlyBase, WormBase). Both are shown for every gene.
Guess phenotypes from PubMed abstracts for unmeasured genes only. Each unmeasured gene takes about 1 minute to read and is capped at 25 genes per run to keep the analysis manageable. Literature-derived predictions are advisory only and do not elevate the overall recommendation above CAUTION.
