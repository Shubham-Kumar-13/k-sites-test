Gold-standard gene lists used by `tools/check_evidence_data.py`.

- `core_essential_CEGv2.tsv`: 684 core-essential genes (CEGv2); `non_essential_NEGv1.tsv`: 927 non-essential genes (NEGv1).
- Source: Hart T. et al., "Evaluation and design of genome-wide CRISPR/SpCas9 knockout screens", G3 7(8):2719-2727 (2017), and
  Hart T. et al., Mol Syst Biol 10:733 (2014). The lists were defined from cell-line screens, independently of the mouse and
  DepMap summaries that K-Sites uses, which is what makes them a fair test.
